I'm a first-year PhD student in physics, working in quantum optics and quantum information. Outside of research I take photographs, usually with a Fujifilm X‑T50, and write essays, thoughts and poems in both Spanish and English. Most of what I care about lives where ideas, poetry, music and physics meet, and I keep coming back to the same companions along the way: Walt Whitman's *Leaves of Grass*, Mark Fisher's writing on loneliness and music, and Phil Elverum's songs as Mount Eerie.
